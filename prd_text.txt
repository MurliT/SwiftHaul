5. Feature Detail
For every feature: why it exists, how it works, and what success looks like.
Feature
Why it exists
How it works
Success metric
Instant driver matching
Businesses lose orders when driver search takes 20+ minutes on WhatsApp.
Geo-radius matching pings the 3 nearest available drivers simultaneously. Radius expands 1km every 90s if no response.
Driver assigned within 4 minutes for 90% of bookings between 9am–8pm.
WhatsApp tracking link
End-customers don't have the app. WhatsApp penetration is near 100% in this segment.
Auto-sends a no-install tracking link via WhatsApp Business API once driver is en route. Updates every 5 minutes.
WISMO calls to the business drop by 60% within the first 30 days.
Automated GST invoicing
Most SMEs generate invoices manually — 15 minutes per order, unscalable.
On delivery confirmation, platform auto-generates a PDF with GSTIN and HSN code. Monthly filing summary included.
Reduces a sales objection present in 40% of leads. Cited in NPS positive responses.
Bulk booking dashboard
Weekend sellers like home bakers need to schedule 10–20 pickups in a single session.
CSV/list import for addresses. Single confirmation screen. One-click assignment to available drivers.
Reduces booking time for multi-order sellers by 70% vs manual WhatsApp flow.
Driver ratings & history
SMEs need reliable driver relationships. Bad drivers hurt customer retention.
Post-delivery 1–5 star rating visible during assignment. Drivers below 3.5 average suspended after 10 orders.
Average order success rate reaches 94%+ vs 74% industry baseline.
Offline booking mode
4G connectivity in Tier-2 cities is intermittent. A dropped booking loses a customer.
Core booking form works offline, stores in local storage, and syncs on reconnection. SMS confirmation always sent.
Zero failed bookings due to connectivity. Reduces support tickets by 25%.
Delivery cost estimator
SMEs need cost visibility before committing at checkout with their customer.
Address-based distance calculation shows estimated cost before booking. Zone-based pricing table always visible.
Reduces booking abandonment by 35%; increases checkout conversion for e-commerce sellers.
Smart notifications
Business owners are busy and can't watch a dashboard all day.
WhatsApp + SMS at key milestones: pickup confirmed, driver arrived, en route, delivered, invoice ready.
Dashboard check-ins reduce by 50%. NPS impact estimated +8 points.
6. Technology Stack
Frontend
React PWA — installable on Android homescreen, offline-first with service workers
Tailwind CSS — utility-first, small bundle critical for ₹8K devices
React Query — smart caching reduces data usage on limited connections
Backend
Node.js + Express — lightweight API layer; easy to hire for in Tier-2 cities
PostgreSQL — transactional data (orders, payments, invoices) with ACID guarantees
Redis — driver geo-location cache and real-time queue for driver matching
Infrastructure
AWS (Mumbai region) — low latency for Chhattisgarh; compliant data residency
CloudFront CDN — fast asset delivery on slow connections
AWS RDS + ElastiCache — managed DB, no DBA headcount needed at seed stage
Integrations
WhatsApp Business API — order notifications + tracking links (100% user penetration in segment)
Razorpay — UPI + Cash on delivery; handles GST invoice PDFs via API
Google Maps Platform — geocoding, distance matrix for cost estimation and driver routing
MSG91 / Twilio — SMS fallback for tracking when WhatsApp is undeliverable

9. Market Opportunity & Why Now
The 18-month window
Post-COVID, Tier-2 city e-commerce grew 3x faster than metros. Last-mile infrastructure has not followed. SwiftHaul has an 18-month window before a well-funded competitor moves in. The lean seed-funded model, SME-first UX, and local driver network are the compounding moats.
Tier-2 e-commerce growth: 3x faster than metros post-2020
Last-mile infrastructure gap: industry order success rate sits at ~74%
SME addressable market: 63 million registered SMEs in India; 8M+ in Tier-2/3 cities
WhatsApp penetration: near 100% in target segment — zero install friction for tracking
Competitive window: 18 months before a funded national player enters Bilaspur and Raipur
